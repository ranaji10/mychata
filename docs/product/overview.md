# Product overview

MyChata ("my cabin") helps the people who share a Czech chata or chalupa run it together: who is there when, what needs fixing, who paid for what, how to hand the place over, where the documents are. Czech first, English second. Users are mostly 45–68, on phones.

## Two kinds of account

|          | Family                                                 | Institution                                                   |
| -------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Who      | Relatives sharing one or more chatas                   | University, union, company or club with recreation facilities |
| Bookings | Members book directly; overlaps are shown, not blocked | Outsiders request stays through a public form; staff approve  |
| Look     | Coral/cream                                            | Teal "professional" theme                                     |

## What exists (V1)

Onboarding · several chatas per account · calendar with overlap and same-day changeover rules · bilingual tasks · expenses with equal/custom/branch splits and settlement · handover checklist with photos and issues · house manual with public share link · document vault with expiry dates · members and invitations · guest booking links · institutional request queue · GDPR consent and data export · PWA with offline cache.

## Market facts we rely on

- Czechs make about 8 million stays a year in their own holiday homes ([Česko v datech, Eurostat 2018](https://www.ceskovdatech.cz/clanek/155-chaty-a-chalupy/)). This non-commercial use is the core segment.
